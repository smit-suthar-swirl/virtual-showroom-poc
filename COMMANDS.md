# BYD Seal Voice Assistant - Voice Commands

Here is a comprehensive list of questions and commands you can ask the BYD Seal assistant.

## 🚗 **General/Visibility**

- "Show me the car"
- "I want to see the BYD Seal"
- "Hide the car"
- "Close the car model"

## 🔄 **Views & Angles**

- "Show me a 360 view"
- "Rotate the car"
- "Spin it around"
- "Show me the front view"
- "Show me the back of the car" / "Rear view"
- "Show me the side profile"
- "Show me from the top" / "Bird's eye view"
- "Go to a showroom angle"

## 🛋️ **Interior (Cabin)**

- "Show me the interior"
- "Let's go inside"
- "Switch to driver's view"
- "Show me the back seats" / "Rear passenger view"
- "Sit in the second row"
- "Go back outside" / "Switch to exterior"

## 🔦 **Highlighting Parts (Exterior)**

- "Show me the headlights"
- "Zoom in on the wheels"
- "Show me the taillights"
- "Check out the side mirrors"
- "Show me the grille"
- "Look at the front bumper"
- "Show me the trunk" / "Boot"
- "Zoom in on the door handles"
- "Show the windshield"

## 💺 **Highlighting Parts (Interior)**

- "Show me the dashboard"
- "Look at the steering wheel"
- "Show me the infotainment screen" / "Display"
- "Zoom in on the seats"
- "Show the center console"
- "Look at the instrument cluster"

## 🚪 **Animations**

- "Open all the doors"
- "Open the bonnet" / "Show me the engine"
- "Open the trunk" (Note: grouped with doors/all)
- "Close everything"
- "Close the doors"
- "Shut the bonnet"

## 🎨 **Colors**

- "Change color to Red"
- "Show it in Black"
- "Make it Blue"
- "What does White look like?"
- "Switch to Silver"

## 🔊 **Sounds**

- "Start the engine" / "Hear the engine start"
- "What does it sound like when driving?" / "Play engine running sound"

## ℹ️ **Information & Trims**

- "Show me the tech specs" / "Performance stats"
- "Hide the specs"
- "Show me the hotspots" / "Enable interactive markers"
- "Compare the trims" / "What versions are available?"
- "Show me the Premium trim info"

## ❓ **General Questions**

- "How fast is it?"
- "What is the range?"
- "Tell me about the battery"
- "Is it safe?"
- "What are the dimensions?"
